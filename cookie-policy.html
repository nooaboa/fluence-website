Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Fluence Agency ("Fluence", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Nooa Adams, uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences or understand how it is used. Some cookies are set by us; others by third-party services we embed.

2. How we use cookies

We only set non-essential cookies after you give consent via our cookie banner. You can accept all, reject all, or choose categories in "Manage preferences". You can change your choice at any time using in the site footer.

Your preferences are stored in a signed, HttpOnly cookie (fluence_consent) set by our server so they persist across visits for up to 12 months, unless you clear cookies or we update this policy (which may prompt you to choose again).

3. Cookie categories

3.1 Strictly necessary

These cookies are required to remember your cookie consent choices. Without them, we would need to ask for your preferences on every page load.

Cookie Purpose Duration
fluence_consent Stores your cookie consent preferences (analytics on/off, policy version, timestamp) 12 months

Legal basis: legitimate interest in providing a compliant consent mechanism (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and where applicable your consent for optional categories stored within this cookie.

3.2 Analytics (optional)

Only placed if you accept analytics cookies. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our website (e.g. pages viewed, approximate location, device type). We do not use this data for advertising or sell it to third parties. No products are sold directly on this website.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Data may be processed in the EU and the United States. Google participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data safeguards.

4. Third-party embeds (contact page)

Our contact page embeds Cal.com for booking strategy calls. When you load or interact with that widget, Cal.com may set its own cookies independent of our banner. We do not control those cookies. Please see Cal.com's Privacy Policy.

5. Other technologies

We load fonts from Google Fonts. This involves transmitting your IP address to Google but does not use cookies in the same way as analytics. See our Privacy Policy for details.

6. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw analytics consent at any time by opening and disabling Analytics, then saving. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings.
