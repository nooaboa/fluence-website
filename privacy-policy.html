Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fluence Agency ("Fluence", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Nooaa Adams, collects, uses, and protects personal data when you visit our website or use our services. We are committed to handling your data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Austrian data protection law.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 When you visit our website

When you access our website, our hosting provider automatically collects the following technical data ("server log files"):

  • IP address (anonymised where possible)
  • Date and time of the request
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL
  • Pages visited

This data is processed on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) to ensure the technical operation of the website. It is deleted within 30 days unless required for security purposes.

2.2 When you book a call

Our contact page uses an embedded booking tool provided by Cal.com. When you book a strategy call, Cal.com collects your name, email address, and any information you provide in the booking form. This data is processed by Cal.com in accordance with Cal.com's Privacy Policy. We receive and store the booking details to fulfil our service and communicate with you prior to and following the call.

Legal basis: performance of a contract / pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

2.3 When you contact us by email

If you contact us directly by email, we process your name, email address, and any other information you voluntarily share. We use this data solely to respond to your inquiry and will not share it with third parties.

Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

3. Cookies

We use a strictly necessary cookie (fluence_consent) to remember your cookie preferences. With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Analytics cookies are only set after you accept them via our cookie banner. For full details, see our Cookie Policy. You can change your choices at any time via in the footer.

Third-party embeds on our contact page (Cal.com) may set their own cookies when you use the booking widget. See our Cookie Policy and Cal.com's documentation for details.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may process your data:

  • Google Analytics — used only if you consent to analytics cookies, to measure website usage. See Google's Privacy Policy and our Cookie Policy.
  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's CDN. When fonts are requested, your IP address is transmitted to Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cal.com — used for booking strategy calls. See Cal.com's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting provider (Netlify) — our website is hosted on Netlify. Server log files are processed on their infrastructure.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. Email correspondence is retained for a maximum of 3 years. Booking data is retained for up to 2 years after the last interaction.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR) — you may request information about the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) — you may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) — you may request deletion of your data where applicable.
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18 GDPR) — you may request that we restrict processing of your data.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) — you may request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR) — you may object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at nooaa@fluence-agency.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde): www.dsb.gv.at.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or destruction. Our website is served over HTTPS.

8. Links to External Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before submitting personal data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
